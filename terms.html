Subscription

Chaguhn offers every feature through a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. When the trial ends, the plan you chose is charged to your App Store or Google Play account and renews automatically at the same interval unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel in your store subscription settings at any time. Refunds follow each store's policy.

Always free

Your child's communication features (talking bar, talking board, switch access) remain available without a subscription.

Limitation

This app is a tool for practising daily living skills and does not replace medical care or therapy. Steps involving knives or heat should always be done with an adult.
